02 — what happened

the confession

No excuses

Just the order of events, so you know I understand what I actually did.

empty, because of me

  1. 01

    The jar was yours

    It was full, it was sitting right there, and it was not mine to open.

  2. 02

    Bhukkad brain took over

    I got hungry in the careless way. I saw churan. I did not see the line I was about to cross.

  3. 03

    One piece became the jar

    The first bite should have been a question. Instead it turned into the whole thing, gone.

  4. 04

    You were left with nothing

    I noticed when it was already empty. You were the one who had to feel it. That is the part I am most sorry for.
